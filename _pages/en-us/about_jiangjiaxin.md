**Jiaxin Jiang**

**Post-doctoral Fellow**

**Affiliation**: School of Aeronautics and Astronautics

**Research interests**: Digital signal processing, event detection and classification, deep learning models, explainable artificial intelligence.

**Brief introduction**: 
He mainly engages in intelligent recognition and classification research based on deep learning and signal processing, focusing on intelligent analysis and feature extraction of seismic signals, satellite imagery, and multi-source data. His research interests include the application of self-supervised learning in seismic event classification, multi-temporal change detection in SAR imagery, and studies on transparency and trustworthiness of explainable artificial intelligence in seismic signal recognition. By integrating learning-based optimization, feature fusion, and model interpretation techniques, he aims to improve the stability and decision reliability of intelligent systems in complex environments.
