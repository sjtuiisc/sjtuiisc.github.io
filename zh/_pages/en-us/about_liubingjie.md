**Bingjie Liu**

**Post-doctoral Fellow**

**Affiliation**: School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing

**Research interests**: AI Psychology and Human–AI Collaboration.

**Brief introduction**: 
From a psychological perspective, this work examines issues of safety and ethics in artificial intelligence, with particular attention to the potential threats and security risks AI may introduce in complex interaction and decision-making contexts. At the same time, it focuses on the psychological responses and decision-making mechanisms that emerge when humans collaborate with AI, exploring how these psychological factors influence the efficiency of human–AI collaboration and the safety of the overall system. The goal is to provide a psychological basis for the design of safe and controllable human–AI intelligent systems.
