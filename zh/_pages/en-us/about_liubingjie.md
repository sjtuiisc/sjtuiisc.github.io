**Brief introduction**: 
With the advancement of deep-space exploration and crewed lunar and Mars missions, the complexity and challenges of space missions continue to increase. Astronauts not only require strong professional competence, but must also collaborate efficiently with AI systems and robots in extreme environments. Space missions involve the integration of multiple systems, and the failure of any one system may lead to mission failure. Astronauts must maintain emotional stability and make efficient decisions under high-risk, isolated, and extreme conditions.

Although AI and robotic technologies have been applied in many mission scenarios, mission success depends not only on equipment performance, but also on the establishment of trust between astronauts and systems, the management of cognitive load, and emotional regulation. At present, despite existing psychological health interventions, how to optimize astronaut–intelligent system interaction and how to understand astronauts’ decision-making mechanisms under stress remain unresolved challenges.
