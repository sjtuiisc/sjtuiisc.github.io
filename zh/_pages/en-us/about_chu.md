**Wenjing Chu**

**Assistant Professor**

**Affiliation**: School of Aeronautics and Astronautics

**Research interests**: Quantum automata learning, formal methods, probability and machine learning theory, quantum information simulation.

**Brief introduction**: She mainly conducts interdisciplinary research on quantum computing and formal methods, exploring the learnability and modeling algorithms of probabilistic and quantum systems. In his doctoral research, he proposed approximate learning methods for quantum automata. Related work has been published at international conferences such as the International Symposium on Theoretical Aspects of Software Engineering (TASE 2023) and the International Conference on Formal Engineering Methods (ICFEM 2022).

In addition, his earlier research focused on simulating quantum nonlocality in optical systems, where he proposed post-quantum correlation experimental schemes based on optical interference. These results have been published in international journals such as Physical Review A, Scientific Reports, and Optics Express.
